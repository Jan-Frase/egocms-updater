# Research

We conduct research and development on parallel systems by developing concepts for high-performance computing, storage
and file systems, as well as for parallel programming. Our main research areas are high-performance computing, storage
and file systems, data reduction techniques, I/O interfaces and programming concepts.
Current Projects

- [SMASH](https://www.parcio.ovgu.de/Research/SMASH.html) (2022-2025)
- [CoSEMoS](https://www.parcio.ovgu.de/Research/CoSEMoS.html) (2019-2023)
- [JULEA](https://www.parcio.ovgu.de/Research/JULEA.html)
